Paste each block below (from ```ansi to the closing ```) as its own message.
The colours use invisible control characters. If they disappear when you paste, post the panels
with /mod action:Post guide panels here instead; the bot posts all 16 as cards (or as these blocks when the newer layout is off).
Panels 1-4 are for players who have never played SEED or New Eridian.
